# Road Map

## Sprint 1
### Week 1-2: UX Research and Requirements 
1. Requirements Elicitation
2. UX Research
   1. Intended users
   2. Demographics
   3. Specific frustrations
   4. How each frustration is taken account of
   5. Surveys/interviews/conversations with friends and Discord members
3. Requirements Specification (discussion)

### Week 3-4: Design
1. Revise Figma Design
2. Decide on software architecture
3. Draw use case diagrams
4. Decide on accessibility

### Week 5-7: Implementation Shallow MVP
1. Decide on frameworks (if any) and tech stack
2. Decide who will develop what
   3. Charles will be in the backend and Shivesh will be in the frontend
2. Implement with pair programming
   1. Driver (writing code)
   2. Navigator (not writing code, giving information and spotting bugs)
3. Refer to architecture and design

### Possible Risks for Sprint 1:
1. Charles is completely new to backend development, and the time lost to learning Spring Boot can delay up to 2 weeks
2. The Spring 2026 semester is about to begin, which can cause further delays.
3. Additional clubs, school work, and other job-related obligations can force us to recalibrate our timeline for future sprints.

### Result of Sprint 1:
1. Decided tech stack to be Flutter for frontend and Spring Boot for backend
2. Succeeded in creating onboarding frontend (Shivesh) and minimal, dummy backend (Charles) 

### Challenges of Sprint 1:
1. Difficulty navigating the complex ecosystem of Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) and migrating from a deprecated library, leading to a loss of one week's time.
2. Charles' journey to learn Spring Boot, a non-beginner-friendly framework, as a predominantly frontend developer led to a 2-week learning period
